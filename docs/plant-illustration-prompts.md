# Regenerated plant illustration prompts

Created 27 September 2026 with the built-in image generation tool, using the corresponding original PNG as a visual style reference for each new PNG. The common style instruction was a scientific educational botanical illustration with clean medium-dark ink outlines, restrained natural watercolor color, an isolated off-white background, a complete specimen centered for the game's portrait card, and no text, labels, watermark, or unrelated plant. These prompts were paired with the species-specific directions below. The [botanical audit](plant-illustration-audit.md) lists the reference descriptions and their sources. Generated images were visually reviewed but have not been validated by a botanist or tested for recognition with users.

| Asset | Species-specific prompt |
|---|---|
| `FENNEL.png` | Wild *Foeniculum vulgare*: a tall branching plant from an unswollen perennial crown, visible fine roots, round green stems, sheathing leaf bases, large finely divided leaves ending in threadlike segments, and several compound umbels of tiny yellow flowers. Avoid a Florence-fennel vegetable bulb. Increase line contrast. |
| `PERENNIAL_PEPPERWEED.png` | *Lepidium latifolium*: complete upright plant with healthy smooth green-gray alternate foliage, larger stalked lower leaves, smaller nearly stalkless upper leaves, clear venation, and branched dense clusters of tiny four-petaled white flowers. Avoid white leaf blotches, holes, variegation, and frilly large flower units. |
| `PACIFIC_CORDGRASS.png` | *Spartina foliosa* / *Sporobolus foliosus*: several upright leafy culms connected by a creeping rhizome. Each narrow head has several close-pressed spike-like branches, not one grain spike. Show the one-sided spikelet arrangement on an enlarged branch and gently rolled upper leaf tip. Avoid a dense isolated basal fountain. |
| `TULE_REED.png` | Hardstem tule, provisionally *Schoenoplectus acutus* var. *occidentalis*: a cluster of tall stout smooth round green culms from a rhizome, reduced basal sheaths rather than grasslike blades, and lateral-looking branched groups of compact brown ovoid spikelets near the tips. A long firm green bract rises above each cluster like a continuation of the culm. Avoid feathery plumes. The exact intended tule taxon still needs confirmation. |
| `SLENDER_ICEPLANT.png` | *Mesembryanthemum nodiflorum*: open low prostrate annual, visible wiry branching stems and nodes, short narrow nearly cylindrical succulent leaves, glistening bladder cells on leaves and stems, just a few small pale flowers on short stalks, and a magnified leaf-and-stem detail for surface cells. Optional red older leaf tips. Avoid a dense ornamental garden-iceplant carpet and oversized daisies. |
| `BRASS_BUTTONS.png` | *Cotula coronopifolia*: fleshy creeping horizontal stems rooting at multiple nodes, alternate leaves with sheathing bases, a mix of narrow entire and a few simply lobed leaves, and several slim flower stalks with rounded yellow rayless button heads. Avoid uniformly fernlike foliage and a single central root crown. |
| `WESTERN_GOLDENROD.png` | *Euthamia occidentalis*: erect stems with many slim linear stalkless leaves approximately 10–25 times longer than wide, subtle parallel lengthwise veins, branching uneven terminal clusters of many small yellow composite heads, and a narrow-veined leaf detail. Avoid broad lanceolate leaves, oversized daisies, and one rigidly flat flower umbrella. |
| `STINKWORT.png` | *Dittrichia graveolens*: complete densely branched annual with many legible alternate narrow leaves, small yellow heads on short stalks near branch tips, and a magnified leaf-and-stem detail showing short glandular hairs and sticky texture. Avoid sparse outlined foliage and large generic daisy flowers. |
