# Arroyo lupine card

Added *Lupinus succulentus* as a California native annual. The illustration shows the features most useful for identifying it: fleshy branching stems, long-stalked palmate leaves with roughly seven to nine broad leaflets, upright blue-violet racemes of pea-shaped flowers with a pale banner patch, and a few softly hairy pods. The card hint describes its annual growth, heavy-soil habitat, and winter-to-spring flowering.

Botanical references: [UC Irvine field description](https://nathistoc.bio.uci.edu/Plants%20of%20Upper%20Newport%20Bay%20(Robert%20De%20Ruff)/Fabaceae/Lupinus%20succulentus.htm), [Calflora taxon report](https://www.calflora.org/app/taxon?crn=5217), and [USDA NRCS seedling guide, page 20](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/plantmaterials/capmctn13726.pdf). The illustration has been visually compared with these descriptions; recognition by players has not been measured.
